# Private market-data snapshot

Raw JSON responses and vendor CSVs are in raw/. Normalized data are in processed/.
market.sqlite is generated by notebook 01 or the pipeline. All market-data files are ignored by Git.
The initial snapshot covers MU and SOXX from 2019-01-02, and current-listing SNDK from 2025-02-24, through 2026-09-30.
reports/manifest.json contains SHA-256 hashes and retrieval metadata. Reuse it to reproduce the saved results; --refresh intentionally replaces it.
Do not place these raw price files in a public repository; obtain your own data under the provider's terms.
